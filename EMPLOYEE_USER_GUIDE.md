# Employee User Guide

Works on phone, tablet and desktop. Bookmark the app URL.

## Sign in
1. Enter your work e-mail → **Send login code**.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your e-mail → **Verify & sign in**. You stay signed in for 12 hours.

## Your dashboard
Today's status (report submitted?), tasks, recorded time, unallocated time, timeline, last 14 days and your open difficulties. Use **Open today's tracker** to start.

## Daily Work Tracker — 4 tabs

### 1. Tasks & timers
* **Add task** → title, category, priority. Leave start/end blank and use the timer, or enter the times if the work is already done.
* **Add & start timer** starts tracking immediately. Only one timer can run at a time — starting another asks to pause the current one.
* **Pause / Resume / Complete**. Completing records the exact duration (e.g. 10:00 → 11:30 = 1 Hour 30 Minutes).
* 🕒 **Add manual time** when you forgot the timer (a reason may be required). Entries cannot overlap each other.
* ⚠ **Report difficulty** on a task: category, level, delay, which department/person you are waiting for, assistance needed, escalation. Approval / other-department / customer dependencies automatically mark the task **BLOCKED** — these delays are never counted against you.
* ✎ Edit · 🗑 Delete · ☰ view time entries.
* Tasks with a status other than Completed need a **pending reason** and ideally an expected completion date.

### 2. Daily questionnaire
* Your questions depend on your designation (e.g. Sales Executive, MIS Executive, Inventory Manager) plus common daily questions.
* Answers auto-save every few seconds. `*` = mandatory. Some questions appear only after a Yes/No answer.
* **Submit questionnaire** when finished (you can still update later unless the day is locked).

### 3. Timeline
Visual bar of your day, first/last entry, recorded vs unallocated time and time by category.

### 4. Submit report
Checklist (questionnaire submitted, tasks recorded, no timer running, pending reasons, unallocated time), employee remarks, **Submit daily report**. Then:
* **Preview report** → Print · Download PDF · Copy text · **Share on WhatsApp** (to yourself, your manager, or any number). On phones, *Share PDF* opens the native share sheet to attach the PDF in WhatsApp.
* You cannot submit twice by accident — use *Update submission* to resubmit after changes.

## After submission
* Your manager may add remarks and approve the day; approved days are locked. Ask your manager to reopen if a correction is needed.
* **Employee Reports** shows your history; **Task Management** lists all your tasks; **Difficulty Tracker** shows your issues and their resolution.

## Tips
* Add tasks as you start them, not at the end of the day — the timeline and productivity figures are built from real timings.
* One task = one activity (e.g. "Quotation – Sharma Interiors"), not "work".
* Record difficulties immediately; they feed the bottleneck analysis that gets dependencies fixed.
